## Mathematical stuff

- [Some Career Advice for Postdocs and Grad Students](https://kimballmartin.github.io/career-ad.html) by Kimball Martin (probably worth revisiting in the age of AI); see also links to other useful resources therein.

- [Pavel Etingof's homepage](https://math.mit.edu/~etingof/), including two essays: "Guidelines for using AI in mathematical research" and "Mathematics of the future".

- [On the great hypocrisy behind “human understanding”](https://proofsandprompts.com/2026/11/11/on-the-great-hypocrisy-behind-human-understanding/): a very interesting article that says things others are afraid to say, even though I don't fully agree with all of its arguments.
